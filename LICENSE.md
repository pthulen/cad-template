## Choosing a licence (delete this section once you've picked)

This template defaults to **All rights reserved**, shown above. Pick one per product:

- **All rights reserved**: for designs you sell. Nobody else may copy, remix or
  sell them. Keep the text above and fill in the year and your name.
- **CC BY 4.0**: for designs you publish for free. Anyone may share, remix and
  even sell them, as long as they credit you. Replace everything above with:

  > This work is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
  > International License (CC BY 4.0).
  > To view a copy, visit https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  > Copyright (c) YEAR YOUR NAME.

The label font in `design/fonts/` is under the SIL Open Font License (see
`design/fonts/OFL.txt`). That allows embedding it in products you sell, whichever
licence you choose above.
